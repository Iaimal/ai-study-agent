Transformers are a neural network architecture introduced in 2017 that rely on a mechanism called attention rather than recurrence or convolution to process sequences of data. This allows them to process entire sequences in parallel, making training significantly faster than older recurrent architectures.

The core idea behind attention is that when processing a word in a sentence, the model can look at every other word and decide how much focus to place on each one, allowing it to capture long-range relationships in text more effectively.

Transformers are the foundation behind large language models such as GPT and Claude. Instead of processing text strictly left to right like older models, they build a rich representation of an entire sequence at once, which is part of why modern language models are so effective at understanding context.
